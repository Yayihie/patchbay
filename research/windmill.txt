Scripts

A function in any of 20+ languages becomes an API endpoint and a form. Windmill resolves its imports, locks versions at each deploy and installs from your private registries.

Workflows: Draw a DAG in the flow editor, or write it as code in Python or TypeScript, and call it as an API endpoint. Retries, approvals and parallel branches are built in.

AI agents: Run on any of 12 providers, or on your own model behind an OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Your scripts and MCP servers become its tools.

Data pipelines: Run DuckDB and Python steps on your own buckets, or import your dbt project as it is: Windmill runs it on your workers and tracks each model as an asset, with its lineage.

Apps: A React or Svelte frontend whose backend is your existing scripts and workflows, each one a function the app calls. Share it with your teams, or embed it in your product.

Infrastructure: Worker groups, autoscaling and instance settings are a YAML file, applied by the CLI or a Kubernetes operator. Self-host in your cloud or on-prem.

Inputs, result and logs: Open a
run
to see what it received, what it returned or why it failed, its logs and its memory, then mark the failure resolved.

Flow timeline: Each step of a
flow
on one time axis: what ran in parallel, how long each step waited for a worker, and how long it ran.

Traces: The HTTP calls a job makes are captured as
OpenTelemetry (OTEL)
spans, with their status and timing, and exported to your own collector: Jaeger, Tempo or any OTLP backend.

Queue and worker metrics: Concurrency and queue delay per worker tag, to see which group needs more workers. Also exported over
OTEL
and exposed to
Prometheus
.

Service logs: The
logs of each worker and server
, searchable from the UI, with the errors of each host at a glance. Also written to stdout, so any log collector such as Fluentd can ship them.

Audit logs:
Who ran, changed or deleted what
, and when, for each script, flow, resource and AI request. Also written to stdout, so any log collector such as Fluentd can ship them.
