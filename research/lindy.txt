Questions before you start.

What is an AI employee?

An AI employee is AI you hand whole jobs to, the way you would a new hire. It works across your tools, remembers how your business runs, and comes back with the work done, which is why people also call it an AI coworker. Lindy is an AI employee for your whole team, in Slack, iMessage, email and your browser.

What is Lindy Teammate?

Lindy is an AI employee for your whole team. Hand it the work you'd give a teammate, like pulling reports, updating your CRM, or clearing your inbox, and it comes back with the work done. It knows your business, so everyone gets time back and the team gets more done.

How is Lindy different?

Most AI tools stop at an answer or a draft. Lindy does the work.

Ask why ad spend doubled and you get a teardown you can open. Ask what customers said about pricing and it answers from every relevant call. Ask for the all-hands deck and you get slides ready to present.

And your whole team shares one Lindy, so what it learns compounds.

How do the free credits work?

$50 in free credits lets you start using Lindy right away.

When those credits run out, plans start at $29.99 per user per month.

How do credits work?

Credits are what Lindy spends when it's working for you, about a cent each, so you only pay for work done, and bigger jobs use more.

Everyone shares one pool. Admins can top up at $10 per 1,000 or set per-person spend caps, and if the pool runs low Lindy pauses with a clear message.

What does Lindy connect to?

Lindy works across your tools, not just next to them. That means Slack, Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Notion, and 3,000+ more.

Any hosted MCP server plugs in too, and if a tool has neither, Lindy can drive the browser like a person would (Pro and up) or build its own integration.
Browse all integrations
.

How long does setup take?

Minutes, with no API keys or IT ticket. An admin installs Lindy in Slack, everyone else links with one tap, and then anyone can @mention it in a channel it's been added to.

Is our data private and secure?

Yes. Your data is never sold or used to train models, and it's encrypted in transit and at rest. Lindy is SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA, and PIPEDA compliant.

DMs with Lindy stay between you and Lindy, shared channels only use public sources, and actions that write or send can wait for your approval.
